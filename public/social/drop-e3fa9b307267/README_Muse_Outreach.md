# Milink — outreach to local businesses (task list for Muse)

Goal: offer GTA businesses with a broken or weak website a **free CHECK** (a short list of website/Google fixes, no obligation). Every message in `Milink-Outreach-Send-List.xlsx` has been reviewed and approved. **Send them exactly as written.** Only change something if it is no longer true (for example, the business has fixed the issue: then skip it).

## Rules (read first)
1. **Send from the Milink outreach email you already use for this. Never from Milad's personal Gmail.** DMs go from the **@milink.ca** Instagram account.
2. **Email signature, on every email (required by Canada's anti-spam law, CASL):**
   ```
   Milad Mohammadi
   Milink · Web design for Toronto & GTA businesses
   milink.ca · +1 (437) 600-3139
   155 Beecroft Rd, North York, ON M2N 7C6
   If you'd rather not hear from us, just reply "no thanks" and we won't contact you again.
   ```
   The mailing address line is **mandatory** on every email (Milad confirmed this address on Nov 5, 2026). If Milad gives you a unit number, add it in front (e.g. "Unit 1234, 155 Beecroft Rd...").
3. Only email addresses the business **published itself** (on its own website, Google profile, Instagram bio, or its own directory listing). Never guess an address (like info@theirdomain.com), never buy lists, never use a personal address you found somewhere else.
4. **Max 5 new messages per day**, weekdays, 9 AM–4 PM Toronto time. Send each email individually (no CC/BCC groups, no mail-merge).
5. **One follow-up only**, 5 business days later, if there is no reply (text below). Then stop.
6. If anyone says no, unsubscribe, or "not interested": reply "No problem, thanks for letting me know!", mark them **Do not contact**, and never write again.
7. Never promise prices, timelines or results, and never mention numbers about Milink (projects, clients, ratings). Only link to milink.ca/work.
8. **Any reply that shows interest** (yes, how much, call me, send it): don't negotiate. Answer "Great, I'll get Milad to send you the CHECK today/tomorrow" and **message Milad right away** with the business name and their reply.

## Step 1 — Send the approved messages (sheet "Send list")
Work from the top (#1 is the best lead). For each row:
1. Open the "Why" link/evidence quickly and confirm the problem still exists (e.g. the site is still expired). If it's fixed, mark **Skipped – fixed** and move on.
2. Use the channels in the "How to reach" column, **in that order**.
3. Email: subject from the "Email subject" column, body from the "Message" column, then the signature.
   DM: paste the message only (no signature).
4. Fill in **Status = Sent**, **Date sent**, and **Follow-up date** (= +5 business days).

## Step 2 — Finding a contact (rows 4–6 say "find an email/Instagram")
Look, in this order, for 5 minutes max:
1. Google the business name + city → their Google Business Profile (website, phone, sometimes email).
2. Their Instagram / Facebook page (bio, "Contact" button).
3. Their Houzz profile → "Contact" / "Send message" button (counts as a published channel).
If you find nothing, move the row to the **"For Milad to call"** sheet with the phone number, and paste the phone opener below.

## Step 3 — "Check before contacting" sheet (4 businesses)
Open their website (or Google their name if there's none) and tick what you see:
- [ ] No website at all, or the site doesn't load / shows "expired" / "not secure"
- [ ] Looks old (copyright year 2020 or older, tiny text on phone, broken images)
- [ ] No way to request a quote (no form, no click-to-call)
- [ ] Spelling mistakes in the business name or page title
If **at least one** box is ticked, write a message using the template below (the first sentence must state the real issue you saw), set **Contact? = yes**, and send it under the same rules. If nothing is wrong, set **no** and skip.
